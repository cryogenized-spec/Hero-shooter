# Hero Shooter — Lore library

This folder contains the **versioned, shareable story bible** for *Hero Shooter*. Documents in this folder are narrative references, not executable gameplay systems. They may reveal important plot twists.

## Character histories

- [Saafiya (“Fay”) — history and continuity](saafiya-history.md): Cape Town upbringing, working life, her relationship with Fayeem, the Russian coercion plot, the developed Copperlane opening and outstanding canon decisions.

## How to maintain it

Record established details separately from proposed scenes. Do not fill story gaps by guessing. Keep the user's creative direction authoritative. Update the character entry when a narrative decision is confirmed, and preserve the distinction between the story's firearm designation and provisional asset names.

The root .gitignore excludes **local lore drafts and temporary notes only**. The finished Markdown files in this folder are intentionally committed to GitHub and will be visible to anyone who has access to the repository. Gitignore does **not** provide secrecy or access control.
