HOME & MORE · CHAPTER 1

Explore the family-run store, visit Mr Chen, and check the shopfront. Mrs Chen's office is on L1 behind its own door; WC 1, WC 2, staff room, receiving and upper family room all have distinct access.

This level uses the original Pine Ridge 2D canvas game systems, not the separate 3D mall scene.
